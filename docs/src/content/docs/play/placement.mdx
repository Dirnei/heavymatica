A **placement** is one schematic version put into your world as a see-through ghost. You can have many placements in a
world. Each one keeps its own position, rotation, mirror and [material swaps](/play/materials/).

Placements are only on your computer. Nobody else sees them, and the server doesn't need Heavymatica.

## Starting a placement

<Steps>
1. Press <kbd>H</kbd> to open the [library](/play/library/), or right-click with the [remote](/play/remote/) at empty
   space while no placement is selected.
2. Select a schematic and, for a cloud schematic, the version.
3. Click **Place**.
</Steps>

The library closes and a ghost of that version appears at your crosshair, selected and **following**. If you are not
holding the remote, a message such as `Hold a Stick to place` tells you to take it. The ghost shows once you do.

A placement always stays on the version you placed. Uploading a newer version later doesn't change it.

## The three states

Every placement is in one of three states. The overlay shows the state as a badge, and the selected placement's
outline has a colour for its state.

| State | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **FOLLOWING** | The ghost sits on the block you look at and moves with your crosshair. |
| **ANCHORED** | The ghost stays where you put it. You can still nudge, rotate and mirror it. |
| **LOCKED** | The ghost is fixed for building. It compares itself with the world and can show only some [layers](/play/layers/). |

## Following and anchoring

A following placement puts its bottom on top of the block face you look at, centred on that block, up to 64 blocks
away. When you look at the sky, it stays where it was. It only shows while you hold the remote.

- **Right-click** with the remote to **anchor** it where it is.
- **Sneak + right-click** on an anchored placement **releases** it. It follows your crosshair again.

While it follows, <kbd>Numpad +</kbd> and <kbd>Numpad -</kbd> raise or lower it against the block you look at. That
offset stays until you anchor it, which helps when you want a build to sit in the ground or float above it.

## Moving

While you hold the remote and the selected placement is anchored:

| Key | Moves it |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>↑</kbd> | One block away from you |
| <kbd>↓</kbd> | One block towards you |
| <kbd>←</kbd> / <kbd>→</kbd> | One block to your left or right |
| <kbd>Numpad +</kbd> / <kbd>Numpad -</kbd> | One block up or down |

The arrow keys follow the way you look, snapped to the nearest of north, east, south and west. Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> to
move 10 blocks per press. Hold a key down to keep moving.

## Rotating

Press <kbd>R</kbd> to turn the placement 90° clockwise, or <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>R</kbd> to turn it counter-clockwise.
It turns around the middle of its footprint, so it stays roughly where it was. Stairs, logs and other blocks turn with
it. This works while the placement is following or anchored.

## Mirroring

Press <kbd>M</kbd> to step the mirror through **off**, **left-right** and **front-back**. The footprint stays where it
is. This works while the placement is following or anchored.

All movement keys can be rebound in Options → Controls. See [Keys](/play/keys/).

## Selecting a placement

Only one placement is selected at a time. The keys, the wheel and the overlay always act on the selected one, and it
gets a coloured outline.

- **Right-click** a ghost with the remote to select it. Its box counts, up to 64 blocks away. If you stand inside a
  box, that one wins; between several boxes you stand in, the smallest wins.
- **Right-click** at empty space to deselect. The placement stays where it is.
- Right-clicking the placement that is already selected changes nothing.

Selecting doesn't work while a placement is following: the right-click anchors it first.

## Locking and unlocking

Lock a placement when it is where you want to build it. A locked placement can't be moved, raised, lowered, rotated,
mirrored, released or removed, so you can't nudge it by accident while building.

**To lock**, with an anchored placement selected, open the wheel with <kbd>V</kbd> and pick **Lock**. Or bind the
**Lock / unlock placement** key in Options → Controls; it has no key by default. Locking also works without the remote
in your hand.

Once locked, the placement starts the [world comparison](/play/comparison/) and you can pick [layers](/play/layers/).

**To unlock**, hold the remote and either:

- pick **Unlock** on the wheel, then **Yes**, or
- press your bound lock / unlock key twice within 3 seconds. After the first press the hints say
  `press again to unlock`.

Unlocking needs the remote in your hand. The placement goes back to anchored and shows its whole ghost again. Its layer
setting is kept for the next time you lock it.

## Removing

| State | How to remove it |
| --- | --- |
| Following | Pick **Cancel** on the wheel, or press your bound remove key once. It goes at once. |
| Anchored | Pick **Remove** on the wheel, then **Yes**. Or press your bound remove key twice within 3 seconds; after the first press the hints say `press again to remove`. |
| Locked | Unlock it first. A locked placement is also removed on its own when it [finishes](/play/comparison/). |

The **Remove placement** key has no default. Bind one in Options → Controls if you want it.

## Ghost opacity

One setting decides how see-through every ghost is: [`[ghost] opacity`](/play/settings/), from 0.05 (barely visible)
to 1 (solid), 0.4 by default.

To change it in game, open the wheel and pick **Opacity**, then a step such as **60%**. The current step is marked. The
change shows on every ghost at once and is saved for the next time you play.

The steps on the wheel come from [`[ghost] opacitySteps`](/play/settings/), by default 20%, 40%, 60%, 80% and 100%. You
can set 2 to 8 steps between 0.05 and 1.

## Big schematics

Heavymatica draws at most [`[ghost] blockBudget`](/play/settings/) ghost blocks at once, across all placements (300,000
by default). Placements that don't fit are drawn as box outlines only, so the game stays smooth. Lower the budget if
your game gets choppy near large builds.

## What the ghost shows

The ghost shows the schematic's blocks as they will be built: chests, beds, signs and banners with their own look,
water and lava, and grass and leaves in plains colours. Signs show without their text. Barriers, light blocks and
structure voids stay hidden.

Blocks your game doesn't know, for example from a mod you don't have, show as missing-texture cubes. The overlay counts
them. Hide them with **Options** → **Unknown blocks** on the wheel, or [`[ghost] showUnknownBlocks`](/play/settings/).

## The block under your crosshair

On a **locked** placement you can point at a ghost as if it were a real block.

### Targeting HUD

Look at a ghost, or at a boxed mistake, and a small HUD under your crosshair shows:

- the planned block's icon and name, such as `Oak Stairs`
- its orientation, such as facing north and half bottom, and whether it is waterlogged
- its state in the comparison colours, such as `Missing`, `Wrong block: Dirt`, `Needs a shovel` or `1/3` for candles
- the placement's name, when it is not the selected one
- `Out of reach` when the ghost is farther than you can reach

The HUD looks through blocks that are already built and stops at the first ghost or at a real block that belongs to no
placement. Ghosts of following and anchored placements, and positions that already match, are not targeted.

[`[targeting] hudRange`](/play/settings/) sets how far away ghosts get named, 32 blocks by default, up to 128. Set it to
0 to turn the HUD off. With [Jade](/play/compatibility/) installed, Jade's tooltip shows the ghost instead.

### Pick block on ghosts

Middle-click (the game's **Pick Block** control) a ghost within reach, and Heavymatica puts the item for the planned
block into your hand. This works on any server.

- If the item is in your hotbar, that slot is selected.
- If it is in your inventory, it is swapped into the hotbar slot that vanilla's pick block would use.
- If you carry a replacement the plan accepts, such as dirt for a grass block, that is picked when you don't have the
  block itself.
- In creative, you get the block even if you don't carry it.
- If the item is only inside a shulker box or bundle you carry, the action bar tells you which container it is in, and
  nothing in your inventory moves.
- If you don't carry it at all, the action bar says `You have no Oak Stairs`.

Sneak while you middle-click to pick the real block behind the ghost instead. Turn ghost picking off with
[`[targeting] pickBlock`](/play/settings/).

## Saved per world

Anchored and locked placements are saved for each singleplayer world and for each server address. When you come back,
they are where you left them, unselected, with their rotation, mirror, swaps, lock and layers. Following placements are
not saved.

Placements from one server don't show on another. A placement in the Overworld doesn't show in the Nether.

If a placement's schematic can't be loaded, for example because you are offline and it isn't cached, or it was deleted
from the library, the placement shows as an outline and the overlay says `Version unavailable: offline or deleted`.
It loads again once the cloud is reachable.
