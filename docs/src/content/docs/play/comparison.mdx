When you [lock](/play/placement/) a placement, it starts comparing itself with the world. Blocks you have built
correctly disappear from the ghost, mistakes get coloured boxes, and the overlay counts your progress.

Following and anchored placements don't compare. They always show their whole ghost with no boxes.

## What you see

Every position of a locked placement is in one state:

| State | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| Matching | Nothing. The block is right. |
| Missing | The normal ghost. Place the block here. |
| Wrong block | A **red** box. A different block is there. |
| Wrong orientation | An **orange** box. The right block, but turned the wrong way. |
| Too many | An **orange** box. More candles, sea pickles or snow layers than planned, or a double slab where a single slab belongs. |
| Not enough yet | The ghost with an **orange** box. For example 1 of 3 candles, a single slab where a double slab belongs, or a flower pot without its plant. |
| Wrong water | A **cyan** box. The block should be waterlogged and isn't, or the other way round. |
| Needs finishing | A **yellow** box. Use a tool or water on it, for example a shovel to turn dirt into a dirt path. |
| Extra | A **magenta** box. A block stands where the plan has air, for example tall grass inside the footprint. |
| Not compared yet | The normal ghost, until the comparison has reached it or its chunk is loaded. |

Boxes follow the shape of the block that is there, so leaf litter gets a flat box and stairs get two. A box only shows
where you can see the block directly, never through a wall. Ghosts don't hide boxes, so you can see a wrong block
through the ghosts in front of it.

### What counts as orientation

Orientation is which way a block faces, its axis, its rotation, top or bottom half, a door's hinge, what a button or
lever is attached to, whether a slab is top or bottom, whether a chest is single or which half of a double chest, and
whether a door, trapdoor or fence gate is open.

Other differences don't count as mistakes: fence and wall connections, redstone power, whether something is lit, a
crop's age, stair shapes, or a barrel standing open.

<Aside type="note">
If you open a gate that the plan has closed, it shows as wrong orientation until you close it again.
</Aside>

### Blocks the plan accepts

Some blocks count as right even though they are not exactly the planned block. For example dirt is accepted where the
plan has a grass block. Where the planned block needs a finishing step, such as a dirt path on dirt or a stripped log
on a log, the position shows yellow until you finish it.

### Positions that are not compared

- Structure voids in the schematic are ignored.
- Blocks your game doesn't know, for example from a mod you don't have, can't be checked. They show the
  missing-texture placeholder (unless you hid unknown blocks) and no box, and they don't count towards progress.
- Positions in chunks that are not loaded are not compared until the chunk loads.

### When there are several problems

When a block is wrong in several ways, you see the first one to fix, in this order: wrong block, wrong orientation,
too many, not enough yet, wrong water, needs finishing. For example dry stairs facing the wrong way show as wrong
orientation first.

## How fast it updates

When you place or break a block inside a locked placement, the positions around it update within a second.

Changes you didn't make, for example by another player, are found by a check that runs in the background, closest to
you first. Near you they show within a few seconds. [`[comparison] cellsPerTick`](/play/settings/) sets how many
positions it checks per tick (20,000 by default). Higher values notice changes sooner but cost more time per tick.

## Layers and the comparison

Ghosts and boxes only show in the [layers](/play/layers/) that are visible. In follow feet, you still see the mistakes
below you. In from-to, mistakes outside the range are hidden.

The in-hand filter only hides ghosts. Mistake boxes show whatever you hold.

## Progress in the overlay

While you hold the remote (or [pin the overlay](/play/remote/)), the overlay shows for the selected locked placement:

- **Progress**: `Built 62%  3104 / 5006`, the matching blocks out of all blocks to build. Air, structure voids and
  blocks your game can't check don't count. Extra blocks don't lower the progress. Not enough yet, and positions not
  compared yet, count as not done.
- **Layer progress**: `These layers 88%  440 / 500`, the same for the visible layers only, when a layer mode is on.
- `Scanning...` right after locking, until every loaded position has been compared once.
- **Mistake counts** for the whole placement, in their colours: `12 wrong` (red), `4 turned` (orange),
  `2 too many` (orange), `2 water` (cyan), `3 extra` (magenta). A count of 0 is left out.
- **Finishing**, in yellow, for the visible layers: for example `3 blocks need a shovel` or `2 blocks need an axe`.
- A **progress bar**: built in green, carried in your inventory in blue, short in orange. See
  [Materials](/play/materials/).

The overlay doesn't describe the block under your crosshair. The targeting HUD under the crosshair does that; see
[Placing and moving](/play/placement/).

## Mistake pointer

On a large build, finding the last few mistakes can take a while. The mistake pointer shows the way to the nearest one.

Turn it on from the wheel: pick **Options**, then **Mistakes**, then one of:

| Choice | Points at |
| --- | --- |
| **Off** | Nothing. This is the default. |
| **Mistakes** | The nearest boxed position: wrong block, wrong orientation, too many, not enough yet, wrong water, extra or needs finishing. |
| **Mistakes and missing** | The same, plus blocks that are still missing. Handy for the last few blocks of a build. |

The setting is saved as [`[targeting] mistakePointer`](/play/settings/) (`OFF`, `MISTAKES` or `MISTAKES_AND_MISSING`)
and applies to every placement.

While it is on and a locked placement is selected, a small pointer under the crosshair shows:

- an arrow towards the position, relative to where you look (pointing down the screen means behind you)
- how far up or down it is, such as `3 up`
- the distance, such as `12 blocks`
- its state in the comparison colours, such as `Wrong block` in red

It only looks at the selected placement and its visible layers. It picks the nearest position as you move and updates
within a second after you fix one. When nothing is left it says `No mistakes`, or `No mistakes and nothing missing`.

## Finishing a placement

When every counted block of a locked placement matches, it **finishes**:

- a short success sound plays
- a toast says `<name> finished` and how to bring it back
- the placement is removed, and nothing is selected afterwards if it was selected

Extra blocks don't stop a placement from finishing, but a part in an unloaded chunk does. If the placement has blocks
your game can't check, the toast says how many were not checked.

If you lock a placement over a build that already matches, it finishes as soon as everything has been compared.

### Bringing it back

For 10 seconds after it finishes, you can get the placement back: open the wheel with <kbd>V</kbd> and pick
**Bring back**, the first segment. With nothing selected you need the remote in your hand to open the wheel. The
placement comes back locked and selected, at the same spot, with its layers, in-hand filter and swaps. Only the
placement that finished last can be brought back.

A brought-back placement doesn't finish again at once. It finishes again after you break and replace a block, so its
progress drops below 100% and comes back.
